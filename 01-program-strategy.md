# 01. Program Strategy

The first-time-buyer readiness program the roadmap feature serves. These are locked decisions, not options.

## Core thesis

Interests are aligned, so the motive is stated out loud, not hidden. Kaleb only wins if these people become qualified, confident, well-financed buyers. Everything that makes that more true (better credit, more down payment, cleaner tax picture) serves both sides. Transparency is the strongest move, not a risk.

## Scope

- **One pathway at launch: first-time-buyer readiness for renters.** Sharpest differentiation, least-served audience, strongest community moat.
- Phase two only, after 2 to 3 real success stories: homeowner-to-investor track and off-market deal service. Do not bundle these at launch.

## Delivery model

- **Leveraged core, thin personal layer.** Genuinely useful, never faked-personal.
- Leveraged and reused: workshop (partly in Amharic), resource vault, group channel, roadmap template.
- Personal and few: one intake session per client, dropping into the group, and the milestone calls.
- Automate everything else: emails, texts, sequences.

## Targets (year one)

- **6 incremental closings** on top of normal business.
- **~50 genuinely engaged people** in the pipeline to produce that (10 to 20% conversion).
- Closings weighted to land in the **3 to 12 month** window. Bias intake toward closer-in buyers for personal time.

## Value posture

- **Generous with the diagnosis.** The roadmap goes to them fully and freely at intake: their number, gap, DPA dollar amounts, three moves. This is the trust-earner and the switching cost.
- **Sequenced on the execution.** Lender intro, DPA application help, tax coordination, credit recheck happen as they progress. Not withheld, just naturally staged. Never tease value to reel people in.

## Dream team (load-bearing partners)

- **Lender** who does the hard cases: manual underwriting, ITIN, non-traditional credit, gift funds. CONFIRM the current one does this. If not, a second lender who does becomes the most important partner.
- **CPA** who understands mortgage underwriting, ideally already serving the Ethiopian business community. Lock before launch. This is a launch gate.
- DPA database and credit roadmap: built once, in-house, maintained by Kaleb.

## Lead source

- Primary: warm outreach to people Kaleb knows, specific referral asks, and community workshops (partly Amharic) at churches and community centers in Clarkston, Stone Mountain, Decatur.
- Referral asks must be specific: "anyone renting who has mentioned wanting to own but figures they cannot yet," not "anyone who wants a house."
- Secondary: existing personal brand content feeds the workshop.
- Ads: off the table at launch. They throw away the community-trust advantage.

## Structure

- **Rolling intake** (people enter whenever they show up, get a roadmap within a week or two, personal clock).
- **Standing monthly group event** for community and accountability, everyone active invited regardless of join date.
- No fixed cohorts. They delay the closer-in buyers and spike Kaleb's workload.

## Touchpoint system (three tiers, routed by time-to-buy)

- **Tier 1 (9+ months / not ready):** fully automated email/text plus group. Near-zero personal time.
- **Tier 2 (3 to 9 months, the core):** automated backbone plus one scheduled human check-in per milestone.
- **Tier 3 (milestone triggers):** the only touches that ping Kaleb personally. Credit target hit, pre-approval, found-a-house. These are exactly when a competitor swoops in.

## CRM

- Pipeline stages: Lead, Roadmap Done, Working the Gap, Mortgage Ready, Actively Shopping, Closed, Long Nurture.
- One routing field: **time-to-buy bucket** (0-3 / 3-9 / 9+). Stage says where they are, bucket says how hard to work them now.
- Bought, not built: Follow Up Boss recommended. The roadmap feature syncs into it, it does not replace it.

## Time protection

- Free value is open to all. Personal time is gated by two filters: honesty-at-intake (3+ years out drops to Long Nurture and automation) and effort (non-movers demote after nudges).
- Exit is always kind and door-open. Nobody is rejected from the free value. They are correctly sorted.

## Open risks

- Hard-cases lender check unconfirmed. Biggest open item.
- CPA is "in mind," not locked.
- Execution consistency on the warm and referral calls. The engine starts there.
- Roadmap must stay a fill-in template completed in 30 minutes, not a research project per person.
- Kaleb is the single point of failure at every milestone trigger. Fine at this scale.
